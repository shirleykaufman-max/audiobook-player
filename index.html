Free up space on this device

1. The tool's copy. Open a book and tap Delete book. This removes the book and its saved place from the tool. The storage line above updates.

2. The downloaded file. When you downloaded the MP3 from Drive, your phone saved its own copy. A web page cannot open or delete it, so delete it yourself:

  • iPhone: open the Files app, go to Downloads, press and hold the MP3, tap Delete.
  • Android: open the Files app, go to Downloads, press and hold the MP3, tap Delete.
  • Computer: open your Downloads folder and delete the MP3.

3. If space still looks used. Clear this site's data in your browser. iPhone: Settings, Safari, Advanced, Website Data. Android Chrome: Settings, Site settings, All sites, find this site, Clear. This removes all books from the tool.
